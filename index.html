Overview

About the Project

“

This chandelier is made of small glowing blocks of resin, a liquid plastic that turns hard when it dries. The blocks are arranged in two rings, one big and one small.

First, one model block is made on a 3D printer. That model is used to make a rubber mould, and resin is poured into the mould again and again to make all the blocks. The frame that holds the blocks is cut by a CNC machine, which is a computer-controlled cutting machine. A light in the middle makes the blocks glow, so the whole chandelier shines softly.

✨
Diffused Internal Glow Frosted block surfaces soften the central lamp into a warm, gentle ambient halo.
🪐
Two Concentric Rings 60 cm outer ring and 40 cm inner ring provide rich dimensional layering.

Fabrication Process

How It Is Made

A step-by-step walkthrough of turning liquid resin and raw sheet into a floating luminaire.

Step 01

3D Printing

Print one model block (the "master") and precision alignment guides (jigs) to help line everything up accurately during assembly.

Master Block
3D Printed Master & Alignment Jigs
Tool: FDM / SLA 3D Printer
Step 02

Moulding & Casting

Make flexible silicone rubber moulds from the 3D-printed master, then mix and pour deep-pour epoxy resin into the moulds repeatedly to cast all the blocks.

Silicone Mould + Deep-Pour Resin
Material: Platinum Silicone & Epoxy
Step 03

CNC Frame Milling

Cut the two rings of the frame using a CNC machine (computer-controlled cutting tool), milling precision pockets to seat the blocks and spokes to mount the central light.

Milled Pockets & Radial Spokes
Tool: CNC Router / Laser Cutter
Step 04

Sanding & Finishing

Sand and polish each block individually: keep a frosted matte back for soft diffused internal glow, and buff the front face to high polish for crystal shine.

Frosted Back
Polished Face ✦
Dual Finish: Diffuse + Reflect
Process: 400 to 2000 Grit & Buffing
Step 05

Final Assembly & Hanging

Fit the finished resin blocks securely into the CNC frame pockets, wire up the central light fixture, test the warm internal glow, and suspend the complete chandelier from a strong ceiling hook.

✦ Tension-balanced hanging cables ✦ Central diffused LED bulb mounting ✦ 360° even ambient glow

Specifications & Engineering

Size and Block Count

Determine how many resin blocks fit around the two rings and calculate the total weight.

📐

The Circumference Sizing Formula

Blocks per ring = (3.14 × Ring Diameter) ÷ (Block Width + Gap)

Ring sizes: Outer ring ≈ 60 cm diameter  |  Inner ring ≈ 40 cm diameter.

Option A

5 × 5 × 5 cm Cubes

Heavy & Bold
  • Block Size: 5 cm width × 5 cm height × 5 cm depth
  • Spacing Gap: 1 cm between blocks
  • Outer Ring (60 cm): 31 blocks
  • Inner Ring (40 cm): 21 blocks
  • Total Blocks: 52 blocks
  • Estimated Weight: ≈ 8.0 kg of resin
Recommended
Option B

6 × 6 × 2.5 cm Tiles

Lighter & Brighter
  • Block Size: 6 cm width × 6 cm height × 2.5 cm depth
  • Spacing Gap: 1 cm between blocks
  • Outer Ring (60 cm): 27 blocks
  • Inner Ring (40 cm): 18 blocks
  • Total Blocks: 45 blocks
  • Estimated Weight: ≈ 5.0 kg of resin
Why Recommended: 3 kg lighter (~38% less strain on ceiling mounts) and 2.5 cm depth allows superior light transmission and easier casting without overheating.
